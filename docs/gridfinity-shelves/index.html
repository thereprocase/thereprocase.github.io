Gridfinity pegboard shelf generator

Open-bottom seats and gussets on the 42 mm Gridfinity grid. A solid backplate joins them to pegs on the 25.4 mm pegboard grid. Downloads place the frame face on the bed.

Array and reinforcement

Gridfinity array · 42 mm pitch
Pegboard mounting

Two rows is the default. One row uses hooks with a 12.7 mm lip below their centers. Taller mounts use top hooks, bottom locking hoops and bearing pegs between; gussets extend to the bottom of the backplate.

Tuning

0° is level, +5° tilts upward, −30° tilts downward. Pegs remain aligned with the board.

Zero uses the reviewed receiver profile. Positive values loosen the seat; negative values can make bins sit proud.

Up to 12 cells per direction, 64 cells total. Large arrays can take several minutes; Cancel stops the calculation. Mounts remain on a 25.4 mm grid, with at most 76.2 mm between columns. The backplate ends flush with the frame. Gussets follow each Gridfinity cell boundary and meet the front beam, independently of the peg columns. Outer mounts remain inside the frame. The 5.4 mm root wall and connector dimensions are fixed.

Fit and print sequence

Print the seat-fit ring first. Check that the bin seats and lifts freely, then test a 1×1 shelf on the intended board. Check straight-up and 30° forward removal before adding contents.

All generated STLs use the inverted frame-down pose. The hook crowns stay below the bed plane. One-row mounts have no lower hoop; taller mounts use the retained upright hoop geometry. Added depth increases leverage at the board; there is no physical load rating.

The earlier 1×1 and 2×1 ASA slices used 0.6 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, three walls, 20% rectilinear, 12 mm³/s and 5,000 mm/s² acceleration, without brim or supports. Those slices predate the solid backplate and mounting-height options. Re-slice all newly generated shelves and inspect the result.

Native CAD and calculation scope

FreeCAD generator source exports native STEP, FCStd and STL with root-wall, connector-preservation and reference-foot checks. Save the parameters above and pass them with --config recipe.json.

The browser generator combines the exported connector meshes with parameterized seat and support solids. It checks connected-solid output, front-beam continuity, reference bin-foot clearance and retained board-side geometry before enabling downloads. This checks digital geometry; it does not measure snap retention, layer strength, creep or installation forces.

Gridfinity receiver dimensions follow Gridfinity Rebuilt. Peg geometry comes from the current peg repository. Sources and asset hashes.
