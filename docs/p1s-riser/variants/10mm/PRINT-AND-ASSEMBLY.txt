Do not mix frames and diffusers from different strip-width kits.
The 24 keepers and rear cable plug are shared across all three sizes.

P1S USB light riser — Draft 09, PETG

PRINT THE COUPONS FIRST
Use fit-coupon-keeper, fit-coupon-diffuser, one retaining-peg-print,
fit-coupon-joint-side and fit-coupon-joint-corner. The keeper coupon is
an actual 24 mm section of the frame. Check support removal, quarter-turn
feel and the 0.15 mm-per-side glue joint before the full frame.
The keys should slide home until the broad shoulders meet, without force.
Clean first-layer flare from the keys if necessary; do not shorten the stops.
Try the vase-diffuser-coupon in white PETG to judge transmission and trimming.

FRAME AND KEEPERS
0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% rectilinear infill.
Print each frame upright, original seating face on the bed. Do not auto-orient.
The overlapping roof seat and receiver details REQUIRE supports in this orientation.
The checked recipe uses normal automatic supports, 40-degree threshold,
0.4 mm XY clearance, 0.25 mm top/bottom gap, 3 top interface layers,
0.5 mm interface spacing, 60 mm/s support and 40 mm/s interface speed.
Supports may start on the part. Critical-only and remove-small-overhang
options are OFF. The recipe uses PETG for both part and supports.
Remove supports while each frame piece is separate, before glue-up. The
inner channel and open-topped receiver wells provide access. Check each
well with one keeper; do not glue the frame until all 24 work freely.
Use retaining-peg-print (flat-sided orientation) for all 24 keepers.
Use rear-cable-plug-print with its flange on the bed. Its open-bottom,
rounded cable notch fits a provisional 3.6 mm cable; two 3 mm holes accept
a small cable tie around the lead. Check the real cable before tightening.

The audit used the owner's regular Polymaker PETG profile, with retraction
guards OFF and XY shrink compensation temporarily at 100% for dimensional
inspection. It does not replace the calibrated material profile. The audit
is not a printer-ready job: select the actual filament, plate and calibrated
compensation when preparing a print. No printer or stored profile changed.

DIFFUSERS
Eight straight strips: four 125.85 x 10.3 x 0.6 mm and four 109.85 x 10.3 x
0.6 mm. The -flat files are the flat-print option.
Vase alternative: print TWO vase-diffuser-front-back sleeves and TWO
vase-diffuser-sides sleeves. Each yields two strips after cutting off the
round returns at their tangent points. Print FOUR vase-corner-diffuser
blanks, one at a time in spiral vase mode.
Vase recipe: 0.2 mm layers, 0.6 mm wall, 25 mm/s outer wall, no infill,
no top or bottom, no supports, 3 mm outside brim with zero brim gap.
Snip the returns, then the brim. On the corner blanks, shallow witness
grooves mark the upper and lower cut lines; trim down their centres.
The corner back surface follows the actual twisted seat. Horizontal bead
width is 0.6 mm; normal thickness varies about 0.544–0.600 mm with tilt.
The upper 1.3 mm laps the sloped roof seat; the raised cam bears opposite
this face. The assembled CAD shows nominal 0.6 mm diffusers. Physical fit and optical
appearance remain to be checked; start with a single corner and keeper.

PARTS
1 each: front, back, left, right, front-left, front-right, rear-left,
rear-right. 24 retaining-peg-print. 1 rear-cable-plug-print.
4 corner diffuser pieces and 8 straight diffuser strips.
Assembly-pose files are references; use the explicit -print or -flat files
where supplied. Print vase blanks only with the vase recipe above.

ASSEMBLY
1. Remove supports and try the keys, all receivers, the cable plug and one
   diffuser. Lightly smooth glass-contact and cable-contact edges.
2. Dry-assemble all eight frame pieces on a flat surface. Close the broad
   joint shoulders, check square, and check the lower seat and glass fit.
3. CA-glue the frame joints sparingly, keeping glue off the LED channel,
   diffuser slots and keeper wells. Let it cure before fitting the tape.
4. Pass the USB lead through the rear tunnel, then install the continuous
   strip. It twists upright over 32 mm approaching each R16 corner.
   Start and cut end meet at the rear recess. Cut only at a marked cut point;
   insulate the exposed cut end. Keep the inline dimmer outside the riser.
5. Fit the slotted rear plug around the lead; use its tie holes for strain
   relief. The 18 x 10 mm tunnel and plug sit 0.5 mm lower than Draft 08
   to clear the roof seat; connector fit is still provisional.
6. Fit FOUR corner diffusers first, then the EIGHT straight sections.
   Move each inward while lowering into the bottom slot, then seat
   it back against the sloped roof. The straight pieces must lower earlier
   as the top edge passes the roof; do not force the taller sheet upward. Insert keepers sideways and turn down to lock.
   They retain the diffuser, not the LED. Do not force a tight keeper.
7. Set the glass on the Z30 seat. The upper lip locates it with nominal
   0.5 mm clearance per side. The glass/seat depth and rear radius still need
   comparison with the physical printer; the viewer body is a reference mock.

EVIDENCE
All eight frame pieces and accessories slice with zero reported warnings.
See dfm-report.json for the updated frame estimates including supports.
Actual material settings will change those estimates.
All four vase input types slice with only outer-wall/brim extrusion and
continuous rising wall paths above 1 mm, with no intervening travel moves.
All eight frames are valid single solids; no frame pairs overlap. Sampled
insertion/keeper checks pass. These are CAD and slicer checks, not a printed
fit test. Nothing has been sent to the printer.
