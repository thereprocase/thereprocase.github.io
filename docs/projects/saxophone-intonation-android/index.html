MUSIC & PRACTICE

BellCurve

An Android practice workspace with intonation analysis, metronome, drone, pitch pipes and a recording deck.

Public source · Android / Expo 56

PROJECT RECORD

Pitch feedback and practice tools in one app. Microphone audio is processed on the device, and recordings stay local until you share them. The source release uses its own application identifier and keeps the third-party audio and font licenses.

What it includes

  • Transposition-aware tuner and per-note statistics.
  • Configurable metronome, drone and pitch pipes.
  • Local recording deck and explicit sharing.
