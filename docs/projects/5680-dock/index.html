Precision 5680 desk dock

D9 P5: a printable laptop cradle with ducted cooling and an adjustable USB-C plug positioner.

D9 P5 engineering prototype · physical fit, loads and cooling remain to be qualified

PROJECT RECORD

The current D9 P5 CAD uses bonded plenum shells, standard wire fan guards, removable H contact cassettes and a polar plug positioner. Inspect the assembly and verification records; the earlier P5 print kit is archived test history.

What it includes

  • Interactive assembly viewer with current CAD and source.
  • Bonded scalloped plenum joints and serviceable fans.
  • Adjustable plug reach with a face clamp and cam support.
