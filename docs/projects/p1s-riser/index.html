PRINTABLE PARTS

P1S USB light riser

A four-piece glass riser with downward-facing USB LEDs, tapered puzzle joints and lift-in diffusers.

R13 update 10 · CAD checks passed; fitting trial and fresh slice next

PROJECT RECORD

An 8 mm LED strip aims down into the enclosure and twists upright around the corners. A rounded shade limits overhead glare; thin diffusers lift into an upper pocket and drop into their seats.

What it includes

  • Four frame quarters with tapered puzzle joints and overlapping seams.
  • Interactive assembly and cutaways, including diffuser insertion.
  • 8 mm STEP/STL parts, assembly CAD and a small joint-fit coupon.
