Gridfinity drawer insert generator

Fit a Gridfinity grid to a drawer outline and split it into printable tiles.

Browser generator · STL tiles in your browser

PROJECT RECORD

Choose the drawer outline, grid quantity and X/Y placement. Divide larger inserts automatically or at selected cuts. Shallow V faces register the glued tiles, with independent X/Y corrections for cross-seam pitch.

What it includes

  • Drawer outline, grid quantity and placement controls.
  • Automatic or manual tile splitting.
  • Print flat side down and glue the seams with a bin holding the grid.
