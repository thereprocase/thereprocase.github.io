Gridfinity pegboard shelf generator

Configure a Gridfinity shelf for quarter-inch pegboard and download the STL.

Browser generator · STL in your browser, FreeCAD source

PROJECT RECORD

Choose the Gridfinity array, seat clearance, reinforcement and angle from 5° up to 30° down. The mounting pegs and board-contact faces stay fixed. Native FreeCAD source is available for editable CAD.

What it includes

  • Array size, clearance, reinforcement and angle controls.
  • STL generated locally in the browser.
  • A small fit ring for dialing in bin clearance.
