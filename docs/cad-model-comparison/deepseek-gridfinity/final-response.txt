## What was built

A 5 × 4 Gridfinity organiser (210 × 168 mm) for M2 / M2.5 / M3 / M4 socket-head screws, hex nuts and flat washers — 13 printable parts: one baseplate and 12 bins.

**Layout** (labels face the front, so you read sizes without lifting):

```
        col 0      col 1      col 2    col 3    col 4
row 3   M3 6-20    M4 6-20    —        —        spare
row 2   M2 6-20    M2.5 6-20  —        —        spare
row 1   WSH M2     WSH M2.5   WSH M3   WSH M4   spare
row 0   NUT M2     NUT M2.5   NUT M3   NUT M4   spare
```

- Screw bins are 2 × 1 with a 1.2 mm divider: **short (6/8/10)** and **long (12/16/20)** compartments.
- Nut and washer bins are 1 × 1, one open tray each — no little holes anywhere.
- Column 4 of rows 0–3 is the extension reserve: four free sockets for M5 or a duplicate bin.

## What still needs a physical fit test

1. **Socket clearance** — 0.15 mm radial is a judgement call; print one bin + one plate cell first and tune `SOCKET_CLEAR_R`.
2. **Stacking lip** — follows the spec, but first-layer squish changes real seating; test a two-high stack.
3. **Label legibility** — 0.6 mm deep at em 8 should be crisp on a 0.4 mm nozzle; a 0.6 mm nozzle will fill the strokes and needs a larger em.
4. **Divider stiffness** — 1.2 mm web under a pile of M4 screws; widen to 1.6 mm if it flexes.
5. **Capacity in practice** — fill fractions are from nominal volumes; real threaded parts pack differently.
6. **Plate flatness** — 210 × 168 × 5 mm may warp; use a brim or split into 2 × 2 tiles if it lifts.

## Known limitations

No lid (carry bins level), no magnet/screw holes (deliberate — the standard foot profile allows adding them), screws split short/long only rather than by individual length, and labels read correctly only when bins face front.
