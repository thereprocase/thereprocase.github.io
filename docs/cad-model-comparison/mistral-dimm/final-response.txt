Close = press down (hooks cam over the wall tops and snap into a groove, 0.75 mm undercut); open = squeeze the two long sides (labelled `PRESS`) ~1.5 mm and lift. Sticks drop in from the top and lift straight out; rounded notches through the end walls let you pinch the bag low.
