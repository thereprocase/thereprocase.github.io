Done. Everything is in `./cadcomp-20261103-fable-dimm`: parametric source, STEP and binary STL per part, README, measured checks, and assembly.json.

**What I built.** A flat two-pocket tray, 150 × 106 × 18.7 mm closed, with a sliding plate lid. Modules lie flat on their heatspreaders, gold-finger edge facing the centre rib, so the contacts sit in the middle of the box with an 8 mm wide relief groove under them. The lid slides in 1.5 mm grooves in both long walls and the closed end wall, so once inserted it cannot be lifted anywhere. It clicks closed over a 0.8 mm triangular detent ridge on the low entrance wall that seats in a dimple under the lid's leading end. A thumb bar 32 mm back from that end is the push surface for opening and closing. Each pocket floor has a 30 × 20 mm thumb slot under the heatspreader, so you push a module up from below and take it by its ends. No hardware, no flexing clips, two printed parts, both print flat without supports.

**Supported envelope.** Modules up to 140 × 45 × 8.5 mm, in a folded 4×6" or 9×18 cm shielding bag, pocket 143 × 48 × 11.5 mm. That covers Kingston FURY Beast and Renegade, Corsair Vengeance LPX, RGB RS and RGB Pro SL, Crucial Ballistix, G.Skill Ripjaws V and all Trident Z variants, and TeamGroup Vulcan Z, with figures from Kingston datasheets, the Corsair support dimension table, the G.Skill FAQ and TeamGroup's spec sheet. It deliberately excludes Vengeance RGB Pro (51 mm), Vengeance LED, Dominator Platinum (56 mm) and Trident Z RGB DC; covering the RGB Pro alone would add 12 mm of width.

**Labels.** Fillaprint at em 10 mm, which is the font's own rule of cap height 14w with w = 0.5 mm: 7.0 mm capitals and 1.0 mm strokes, above the 5.88 mm minimum for a 0.42 mm line. Lid: `DDR4 DIMM ×2` and `OPEN →`, raised 0.6 mm. Pocket floors: `CONTACTS ↓` / `CONTACTS ↑` and `MAX 140×45×8.5`, debossed. Underside: `MAX 140×45×8.5 mm`.

**Verified on the exported meshes** (checks/CHECKS.md, checks/checks.json): all parts watertight; lid 101.5 mm in a 102.0 mm groove, 2.4 mm plate in a 2.7 mm groove, captured 1.25 mm per side; reference 140×45×8.5 modules do not penetrate base or lid, with 3.0 mm left above them for padding and the contact strip 4.84 mm above the relief groove floor; the lid slid through its full 148 mm travel clashes with nothing except the detent ridge lifting it by at most 0.8 mm as intended; 7.0 mm capitals measured on the lid mesh.

**What still needs a physical test.** The detent click force, which depends on how much the 2.4 mm plate bows on your printer and material; the 0.25 mm per side and 0.3 mm vertical sliding clearances; whether your particular bag folds neatly enough to fit the 48 mm pocket; and the 1.5 mm groove roof overhang. All of these are single parameters at the top of `cad/dimm_mailer.py`.

**Limitations stated plainly.** This is an inner tray and relies on the outer carton plus your padding for crush protection. The thumb slots are open holes in the floor. The lid can be inserted backwards by mistake; it still closes but does not click. Section images I used for my own verification were kept out of the deliverables as requested.
