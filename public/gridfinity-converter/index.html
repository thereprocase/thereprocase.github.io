Screw holes fit within the available floor by default. The result shows the actual depth; choose Exact depth under Hardware dimensions to require a fixed depth.

Export options, checks and format details

Mesh output · STL and 3MF

Default export uses the detected base-down orientation. 3MF contains the selected part and a conversion report, without printer settings. STEP and BREP inputs preserve CAD surfaces through native solid operations and provide validated BREP export. STEP translation is validated separately when requested. CAD mesh exports start at 0.01 mm deviation and adapt automatically for large models; the actual value is recorded in the conversion report. Source colors, textures, assembly structure and CAD feature history are not retained.

How detection works and its limits

This first version handles male Gridfinity feet on individual parts. Female sockets, baseplates, grids and racks are outside this converter’s scope.

The detector compares three horizontal cross-sections with the 0.8 mm chamfer, 1.8 mm straight section and 2.15 mm upper chamfer. It fits matching centers to a 42 mm lattice. A single foot can be recognized from its profile, but cannot independently establish grid pitch. Low-confidence or off-grid features need review; a regular flat block is not automatically treated as a Gridfinity foot.

The conversion replaces selected foot regions and cuts the requested hardware holes. It verifies a connected, closed mesh and measures any change outside those regions. Existing geometry above the seam is retained, except for the requested hole depths; old blind cavities above the seam can remain enclosed. Check the preview and slicer toolpaths, and print a fit test before converting important parts.

STEP and BREP stay as native boundary representations during conversion; tessellation is used for detection and display. IGES currently follows the mesh conversion path. Open, self-intersecting or inconsistently wound surfaces can be viewed but must be repaired before conversion. Check units on STL, OBJ, PLY, OFF and FBX. Skinned models need a baked static export. Proprietary project files such as .blend, .f3d and .sldprt must first be exported to a supported format.
