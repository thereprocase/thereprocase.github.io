Where the glass sits

The glass rests on the broad perimeter seat at Z34 mm, inside the outer retaining lip. That lip rises another 2.5 mm to locate the glass. The roof’s sloped seating face overlaps the diffuser’s upper edge. A bottom slot supports each diffuser; two quarter-turn upper keepers hold it against the roof seat.

The installed views add a dark-grey mock of the printer’s upper enclosure and blue translucent glass with a black 17.5 mm border. Glass thickness is assumed to be 3 mm for these views. Assembly and access

  • Four corners carry locating tongues. Four straight members contain matching sockets with 0.15 mm clearance per side. Broad shoulders set the frame dimensions.
  • Dry-fit on the printer and check the glass. Remove the frame and CA-glue it on a flat surface.
  • Print the frame upright, with the original seating face on the bed. The upper lips and receiver overhangs need supports. Remove them from each separate piece before glue-up.
  • Install the continuous strip after glue-up. It twists upright before each rounded corner, avoiding a forced sideways crease. The gold tape is an 12 mm × 0.8 mm reference; check the real strip’s thickness and bend before installation.
  • The rear tunnel is provisionally 18 × 10 mm. A removable slotted plug closes around the cable after its connector passes through.
  • Start and cut end meet beside that entry. Keep the supplied touch dimmer outside the enclosure.

Vase diffuser option

A shallow oval sleeve gives two straight strips when you snip off the rounded ends. Print two front/back sleeves and two side sleeves. Print four corner sleeves, one at a time; their shallow trim grooves mark the finished edges.

Use 0.2 mm layers, a 0.6 mm wall, 25 mm/s outer-wall speed, no top, bottom or infill, and a 3 mm outside brim. All four vase blank types have been sliced and checked for continuous rising wall paths. The corner wall is about 0.54–0.60 mm thick normal to its surface. Try the small white-PETG coupon before making the full set.

Overlapping roof seat and cam keepers

This kit uses a 14.3 mm tall diffuser. Its upper 1.3 mm overlaps a continuous sloped face under the roof, closing the previous sightline into the light channel. The rounded cam nose moves up 1.5 mm to bear directly opposite this face. The roof supports the back of the sheet instead of leaving its top edge free.

The CAD fits a nominal 0.6 mm sheet between the seat and cam without crushing it. Actual grip depends on the printed wall thickness; test the keeper coupon first. The corner vase wall varies with its slope, so check one trimmed corner too.

Install the corner diffusers before the straight pieces, with keepers removed. Bring each inward, lower it into the bottom slot, then settle it back against the roof seat. For the tilted straight pieces, lower slightly earlier as the upper edge passes the roof. Fit and turn the keepers down afterwards.

The Twist-lock detail shows the actual section: ivory diffuser, teal roof seat, orange cam and gold LED behind the sheet. The 24 keeper locations, lower slots, glass height and upright print orientation are retained.

Printing and assembly

Frame recipe: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, three walls and 15% infill. Normal supports use a 0.25 mm Z gap, 0.4 mm XY clearance and three top interface layers. Keep the frame upright. The four sides and four corners occupy two plates; their combined estimate is about 19 h 37 min and 414 g, including supports, with the audit profile.

The rear tunnel has an 18 × 10 mm clear passage. The plug has an open, rounded cable notch and two holes for a small cable tie. Print rear-cable-plug-print flange-down. Use retaining-peg-print for the 24 flat-sided keepers. Dry-fit on a flat surface, clean up the supports, then glue the frame before installing the tape and diffusers.
