Locate it, then slide it home

At the insertion pause, lower the assembled tool into the opening about 12 mm inward along each axis. Rest the feet on the bed, then slide outward until they register against the printed rim. Removal reverses that movement.

Lowering straight into the final position would catch the feet on the LED backing. The checked route enters farther inward first.

Height and release coating

The support follows the full visor footprint with a 2 mm design border. The checked minimum stays above 1.5 mm with ±0.2 mm placement play across all four quarters. The small sliding clearances at the puzzle joints remain.

The bare tool is 14.44 mm tall, 0.06 mm below the CAD visor. The feet have about 0.20 mm side clearance; puzzle keys have 0.10 mm radial clearance.

Use heavy glue stick on the top contact faces, let it dry, and check the coated height. The film can be thicker than the bare gap.

The final height and insertion pause need checking against the sliced layers and printhead travel. The geometry checks pass; coated release and reuse still need the coupon trial. No probing or re-zeroing is part of this method.
