A lower, simpler frame

Four quarters meet at tapered puzzle joints with overlapping seams. The joints are intended to hold without glue; the small coupon lets you check the fit first.

  • 24.5 mm overall height; glass seat at 22 mm.
  • 387 × 355 mm glass pocket.
  • 8 mm LED strip, aimed 45° down on straight runs and upright around the corners.
  • Rear cable tunnel with a removable plug.

Lift-in diffusers

Lift the diffuser into the upper pocket, move the bottom over the rounded slot entrance, then lower it into the seat. The entrance has no flat shelf to catch the thin edge. Small CA dots at the hidden upper contact are optional.

The straight diffuser face lies at 30° above horizontal, flatter than the LED backing. It is 0.6 mm thick, with 1.6 mm of insertion lift. Assemble the frame before fitting the strip and diffusers.

Printing

The frame prints seat-down. PET-GF15 is the current material choice; PETG is an alternative. A reusable visor-support fixture is being evaluated. Bed-starting edges use 30° chamfers from vertical (60° from the bed). The higher edges that the PTFE tube touches stay rounded.

Use your tuned machine, filament and process profiles. Plan organic supports from the build plate only under the broad, flat visor underside. Check the support contacts in the preview before printing.

The new frame and thin diffusers still need a fresh slice and fitting trial. The download contains geometry, not a pre-sliced print job.

Light into the enclosure

The shade hides the LEDs from steep overhead views while allowing shallow upward spill around 20–25°. Opening the lower edge gives more of the downward beam a clear path.

The section model passes about 69% of the assumed light output downward, compared with 56% in update 6. This is a geometric comparison, not measured brightness: diffuser scattering, absorption and reflections are not modelled.

Geometry checks

64 assembly checks and 12 diffuser lift positions pass. The LED backing, glass pocket and cable plug clear their checked boundaries. All 21,125 sampled seam sightlines are blocked; all 1,300 sampled 45° downward rays clear the frame.
