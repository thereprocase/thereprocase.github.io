KRAKEN FOR THE BAMBU LAB AMS · CARDBOARD SPOOLS

Kraken spool ring generator and nut remixer

Centering rings that seat a cardboard spool on the Kraken spool holder, sized to the spool's centre hole from 45 to 95 mm in 0.1 mm steps, and a Kraken spool nut with a thicker flange. The STL files are generated in your browser.

Kraken is designed by humebeam. These rings and the nut are remixes of humebeam's originals. Every ring keeps the holder core (bore and snap ribs) from humebeam's matching kraken-spool-ring-NN-mm file, and the nut is humebeam's nut with material added to its outer face. 01 / WHICH SIZE TO PRINT

Measure the spool's centre hole with calipers in a few directions, take the smallest reading, and print that size or up to 0.9 mm below it.

The round support surface sits 0.1 mm inside the nominal radius, so a ring larger than the hole will not enter. The five pentagon points stand 0.7 mm proud of the nominal radius, so they still grip a hole up to about 1 mm wider than the nominal size. Print one gear-side ring and one cap-side ring per spool.

Fit adjustments

Defaults 0.7 and 0.1 mm match every published pair. A larger bite grips a looser hole harder; a larger undersize eases a tight hole. Diameters change by twice these values. File names record non-default values.

04 / NUT REMIXER · THICKER FLANGE

Thickens the flange of humebeam's spool nut on its outer face. The bearing face, barrel, thread and grip outline keep their original facets, so the same wrench fits. The added slab continues the single-start 6 mm trapezoid thread exactly one pitch, and the thread lead-in chamfer and bed-edge chamfer are re-applied at the new outer face.

1.5 mm is the original. Default 3.5 mm. Up to 7.5 mm, where the one-pitch thread copy ends.

05 / HOW THE FILES ARE MADE

Rings

The browser repeats generate_family.py boolean for boolean with the vendored Manifold engine. The outside is a 256-segment support circle joined to a five-point pentagon (first point toward +Y), with a 2 mm lead-in that pulls the points in by 1.9 mm. Inside a cylinder of up to 22.5 mm radius, the ring is humebeam's original holder core for the next smaller whole size. The cap side keeps that stock ring's 0.4 mm lip; the gear side gets a 1.5 mm flange reaching 10.25 mm past the hole radius, and its spigot rises 1.1 mm so it inserts as deep as the stock ring.

Checked against the Python generator's own files at 45, 46, 50, 53.4, 55, 55.5, 60, 70, 80 and 95 mm: the largest symmetric volume difference is 7.4 × 10⁻⁵ mm³ on parts of 5,000–74,000 mm³.

Nut

The nut follows the 3.0 mm stiff-nut builder with the added thickness as the input. Against that builder's 3.0 mm STL the symmetric volume difference is 1.5 × 10⁻⁴ mm³. Each extra millimetre of flange takes one millimetre from the widest spool the holder accepts, and adds about 7.3 g of PETG at full density.

Downloads are checked as one closed solid at their float32 STL coordinates before the buttons enable. A 53.4 mm pair and a 3.5 mm nut sliced without supports or brim in OrcaSlicer with P1S PETG profiles. Physical fit of generated fractional sizes has not been measured.
