Tile layout

Usable area should already exclude purge lines, bed exclusions and any brim. Tiles keep their X/Y orientation; automatic tiling reserves room for the V faces.

Cuts can cross seats. Closed floors or solid seam strips in Open mode keep tiles connected; every exported tile is checked. Manual oversized tiles are flagged and downloads remain available for deliberate larger-bed layouts.

Seats and glue joints

Open removes the center of each cell floor while retaining the seats, outer frame and at least 2.4 mm of floor beside each glue face. Closed is the default.

Start with zero correction. Positive stock adds half the value to each opposing face; negative stock removes it. This changes seam pitch when assembled, so use it only to correct a measured print/glue error. It does not alter the 42 mm pitch inside a tile.

Joint and grid-pitch calibration

The default is an assumed 0.10 mm horizontal CA bondline and a 0.8 mm V depth. These are starting dimensions for a fit trial, not measured glue or printer tolerances. The V runs through Z and is complementary across each X or Y cut. It has no dovetail, snap or positive lock. Both tiles remain supported by the drawer floor.

Keep bins or another 42 mm reference spanning a seam while the adhesive cures. Do not simply squeeze every joint fully shut: consuming the intended glue gap shortens the grid across that seam. With no print error, the cross-seam pitch error is face-stock correction + actual bondline − assumed bondline. Positive stock increases the assembled pitch. The same correction is added at every seam in that direction and accumulates across the drawer.

For calibration, download the seam-trial recipe and load it above, or generate a 2×1 insert with one manual X cut at its center. Print both halves in the intended material and pose, check a bin spanning the seam, and measure before committing a full drawer. Keep adhesive off the bin. Repeat in Y if its measured fit differs. The plan and assembly view show nominal grid registration; they do not simulate squeezing or adhesive thickness.

Geometry and printing scope

Each tile is a watertight, connected solid with a 1.2 mm default floor thickness and 5 mm seat region. Closed mode keeps the entire floor; Open mode has 32.5 mm rounded cell windows, with seat ledges and solid glue-edge floor strips retained. The receiver profile follows the reviewed pegboard generator; added seat clearance is independent of drawer edge padding and joint tuning. Downloads place the flat underside at Z=0. No supports are intended for the shallow seams, but inspect the toolpaths, first layer, pockets and seam edges in your slicer. No filament or printer settings are changed by this page.

Generation and STL export run locally in your browser using Manifold. Large arrays take longer; Cancel stops the worker. The generator checks connected solids in either bottom mode, closed mesh edges and reference bin-foot interference before enabling downloads. Those checks do not certify physical bin fit, drawer dimensions, glue strength or shrinkage. Dimensions and assembly notes · Gridfinity reference.
