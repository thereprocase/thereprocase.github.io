ASA ADHESION AND WARP
Use the established enclosed ASA setup, clean textured plate, heat soak and
stable surroundings. Avoid drafts. Let the complete plate cool naturally in
the enclosure before removal. The 8 mm brim helps adhesion but cannot guarantee
that a 245 mm tall body or its locating lands stay straight.
Do not use screws to pull a bowed part into shape. Do not boil or anneal these
dimension-sensitive parts as an uncalibrated repair. Inspect cooled flatness
and fit first; any corrective shrink or geometry change needs new measurements.

PHYSICAL ACCEPTANCE
1. Print the small 84 mm paired-key coupon plate in the intended ASA/profile
   first where practical. Both keys must enter together and both zero-gap tapers
   must seat by hand with shoulders flush. Keys have 4 walls/100%; sockets 4/30.
   Pitch is along X, matching the final bodies and bars. The coupons do not
   reproduce full-height warping, full-box retention, or every layer phase.
2. Fully cool the bodies and bars. Inspect for lifting, cracks, displaced feet,
   distorted seating lands, rough socket mouths and sagged roofs. Clear loose
   strings only; do not force hardware through the thin roller blind stops.
3. Dry-fit halves, hardware and routed cables with bars absent. Set the assembly
   loosely into the box, seat both bars fully, then engage tabs and fasteners.
   Screws must start normally and shoulders must meet without forced bending.
4. Confirm cable slack, connector access and PTFE routing. Actual flexible cable
   bundle dimensions and a complete AMS box solid were unavailable; test these
   physically before final tightening.
5. Add the intended spools progressively. Rotate and feed in every bay; verify
   no rubbing, seat movement, cable trapping or incomplete bar capture. Recheck
   seating and permanent movement after 24 hours under intended service load.
   Carry a loaded tub by its supported body, not front bars, keys or feeders.

NATIVE 0.05 mm acceptance does not promise 0.05 mm printed accuracy. The above
checks define acceptance of the first print; they are not a lifetime rating.
