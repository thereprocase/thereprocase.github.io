**Mechanism**  
- The lid snaps over the 0.5 mm ridge on the base top edge (no hardware, purely elastic flex).  
- To open, press the latch outward, releasing the snap; the lid lifts off cleanly.  
- Sticks are placed in individual cavities, each wrapped in a static‑shielding bag; a thin foam sheet (≈2 mm) can be inserted between them to prevent rattling.

**README (excerpt)**
```
# DDR4 Mailer – Reusable Clamshell Box
Two‑part box for shipping two DDR4 DIMM modules with ordinary heat spreaders.
- No metal hardware; lid snaps onto a thin ridge.
- Adjustable foam spacer to prevent rattling.
- Designed for standard 133 mm DDR4 sticks (width up to ~34 mm). Larger RGB heatsinks are out‑of‑scope.
- Font used for optional label: Fillaprint-Regular.ttf (minimum printable size 2 mm height).

## Files
- `base.step` / `base.stl` – bottom half.
- `lid.step` / `lid.stl` – top half with latch.
- `assembly.json` – part filenames with their transformation matrices (mm).
```

**Limitations / What Still Needs Physical Testing**  
- **Snap‑fit durability:** The 0.5 mm ridge and 2 mm latch thickness rely on material flex (assumed PETG/PLA). Real‑world testing will confirm the number of cycles before wear.  
- **Foam spacer fit:** The 2 mm gap works for typical thin foam or paper; thicker padding may need the cavity clearance increased.  
- **Label embossing:** Font rendering was disabled due to environment limits; the model can easily re‑enable it once font handling is resolved.  

**Hand‑over**  
All source code (`mailbox_design.py`), exported STEP/STL files, README, and assembly description are ready in the `output/` folder.
