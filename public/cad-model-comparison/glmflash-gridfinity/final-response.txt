## What was built

A 5×4 Gridfinity patch (209.5 × 167.5 × 5 mm original-style baseplate, dovetail sockets per the official Design Reference v5) plus **19 labelled 1×1 bins** and one reserved empty slot. Parametric source in `src/organizer.py` (build123d); one script builds all 20 parts and writes `assembly.json` with the assembled transforms (mm, origin = plate underside centre, front = −Y).

**Starter assortment** (rows front→back, columns left→right):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| front | WASHER M2 | WASHER M2.5 | WASHER M3 | M3 x 12 | WASHER M4 |
| | NUT M2 | NUT M2.5 | NUT M3 | M3 x 16 | NUT M4 |
| | M2 x 6 | M2.5 x 6 | M3 x 8 | M4 x 10 | M4 x 20 |
| back | M2 x 8 | M2.5 x 10 | M3 x 10 | M4 x 16 | *extension slot* |

All six requested lengths covered; only **three bin types** (3u/4u/5u, 25.4/32.4/39.4 mm tall) differ by nothing but height and label.

**Labels:** engraved 0.5 mm with the supplied FilaPrint font on two faces — inner back wall (readable from above for *every* bin while seated; column heights are ordered so nothing occludes) and outer front wall (front row / in hand). Cap heights 4.3–5.0 mm, wording spells out NUT/WASHER.

**Interfaces & clear spaces** (verified numerically in `checks.json`): bin body 41.5 (0.25/side grid clearance) → exactly 0.500 mm gap between assembled neighbours, zero overlap; dovetail pad/socket straight fit 0.25 mm/side; bins rest 0.1 mm high on the 0.1 mm socket floors; stacking socket bore 37.7 with 0.75 rim ring — stacked bins never touch contents.

**Capacities (geometric, measured):** every screw bin holds ≥ 20 screws standing head-down (23 for M4 … 68 for M2) with the stack fully below the rim; 30 nuts or 30 washers per bin stay a 0.5–6.4 mm pile. All 20 STLs binary + watertight; all 20 STEPs re-import.

## Handling

Read sizes looking down into the drawer; pinch a screw's shank and pull it up; washers/nuts are top-layer picks; lift bins straight out to carry (nothing spills past the rim); stack for transport only.

## Still needs a physical fit test

The capacities are geometric bounds, not drop tests. Print one bin first and check: ① bin↔plate fit at the 0.25 mm/side fit (wax or ease if your printer runs tight — don't sand the bin pad, it's the stacking interface too), ② cheap M4 heads >7.0 mm eat the 23-screw margin, ③ M2.5 nut assortments that ignore ISO 4032 (AF 5.0), ④ non-standard washer ODs, ⑤ stacking fit against other vendors' bins, ⑥ legibility of the densest label ("WASHER M2.5", 4.3 mm cap), ⑦ drawer depth ≥ 40 mm and a 210 × 168 mm patch.
