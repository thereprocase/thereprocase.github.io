**What it is.** A two-part printed mailer (base + snap lid), 149.3 × 112.3 × 16.7 mm assembled, holding two bagged DDR4 UDIMMs lying flat in separate recessed pockets. Sticks ride only on their bagged heatspreader faces; the gold-contact edge always faces a void — ≥1.2 mm to any rigid wall in the worst jammed pose, plus 8 mm relief grooves in each pocket floor under the contact-adjacent face strips. ESD protection comes from the bags; the box is purely mechanical.

**Lid and access.** The lid is a cap retained by four 0.7 mm wedge nubs snapping into grooves in the base wall (measured 0.45 mm release deflection, 0.25 mm skirt gap, ~0.1 % skirt strain); its ceiling lands on the base rim so sticks can't be crushed. Open by pinching the lid's end scallops against the base's end grip-ribs (arrows on the lid point at the lift ends). Sticks come out via a 7 mm finger scoop per pocket plus a divider notch — 5.4 mm of grip height; straight-lift and 18° tilt removal are collision-free in simulation. A foam/paper sheet on top kills rattle.

**Envelope (researched, not guessed).** L ≤136, H ≤45, T ≤8.5 mm, plus 1.2 mm/face bag allowance.
