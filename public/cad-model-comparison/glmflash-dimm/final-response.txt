## Handover

**What was built** — a parametric two-part mailer (`mailer.py`, build123d) for two heatspreaded DDR4 UDIMMs: **144.8 × 30.6 × 49.9 mm** outer. Sticks stand vertically, **spreader edge down, gold-contact edge up** — the pads face into a 1.65 mm air gap under the closed lid, drops load through the spreader edge, and no part of the box can ever touch them (verified geometrically for every SKU, bare and bagged).

**How the lid stays on / comes off** — each long wall has a lid track whose floor rises toward its mouth (a dovetail undercut); the lid's stepped edge rails are taller than the track mouth, so it cannot be lifted (jams at +0.30 mm), tilted (jams immediately) or pulled sideways. A tooth under each rail drops into a latch pocket at the back wall — a felt click at closed; pulling the tab lifts it ~0.2 mm over the stepped lip and the lid slides fully out the front (removal is the loading method). Sticks then lift straight up. A `CONTACTS ↑` FillaPrint label is engraved in each slot floor, read at the moment of loading.

**Envelope, from researched real products** — ≤ 134.5 × 42.5 × 9.0 mm per module: verified against Kingston FURY Beast DDR4 (133.35 × 34 × 7.2, official datasheet in `research/`), Corsair Vengeance LPX (34 mm official), G.Skill Ripjaws V (42 mm official), Patriot Viper 4 (42 mm), Vulcan Z (~32 mm). Deliberately excluded: Trident Z RGB (~44.5), Vengeance RGB Pro (44.45), Dominator (56). The three parameters at the top of `mailer.py` regenerate the whole design for anything else.

**Labels** — FillaPrint glyphs (OFL font as supplied): `2 × DDR4` on the lid at size 12 (1.22 mm strokes), `CONTACTS UP` in the slot floors at size 10 (1.02 mm) — the font's minimum practical size, measured from its own glyph geometry (≈0.10 mm stroke per point); pockets 0.8 mm deep for inlay filling.
