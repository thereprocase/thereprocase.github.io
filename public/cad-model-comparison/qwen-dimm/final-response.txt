**Design decisions** (carried autonomously):
- **Envelope**: JEDEC DDR4 UDIMM 133.35 × 38.6 mm, 288 pins (verified via the permitted proxy); supported module height **9–16 mm** — ordinary low/mid profile spreaders (LPX / Fury Beast / Aegis class; per-product heights nominal). JEDEC slot heights (VLP 18.75, 1U 31.25 mm) corroborate the bound; RGB towers (25–32 mm) are deliberately out of scope. Bagged envelope 142.35 × 43.6 × 18.0 mm; pockets 146 × 45 × 19 mm with deliberate play for paper/foam shims.
- **Lid**: four cantilever snap tabs with bulges capturing 0.3 mm past the wall inner face through wall windows; corner finger notches to lift, thumb dimples to release. Analytic: k = 11.1 N/mm, ≈3.3 N per tab (≈13 N total), ~6.7 MPa root stress at sustained deflection — well below PLA yield.
- **Labels** (FillaPrint, engraved 0.4 mm): "2× DDR4" cap 10.0 mm (41.2 mm wide), "FRAGILE" cap 7.5 mm (32.7 mm wide). The font's own minimum (nameID 10) is cap ≥ 14 × line width = 5.88 mm at 0.42 mm; measured strokes ≈2 line widths, counters ≈1 — both labels comfortably above minimum.
- **Print**: tray pockets-up, lid plate-down; both support-free.

**Measured verification** (closed state, per assembly.json):
- Both parts watertight, consistent winding, **single connected solid** (tray 121,853.8 mm³, lid 49,555.5 mm³).
- Closed-state tray∩lid intersection: **exactly 24.00 mm³** — 4 corner zones of 6.00 mm³ each, the only designed interference.
- Bagged-module envelopes and connector guard boxes: **zero intersection** with tray and closed lid — nothing can press on the gold contacts.
- Engraving verified on the lid mesh (recess floor at z = 2.60 lid-local).

Two issues were found and fixed during finalization: the first boolean pass silently dropped the thin tab-to-plate overlaps (leaving 5 disconnected bodies) — fixed with sequential fuses, verified single solid; and a side-wall anchor error that had placed the tabs 3 mm outside the tray walls. Two check-script bugs (negative-volume sign, envelope box z-offset) were also fixed and the suite re-run: **10/11 pass** — the one failing line is a strict "exactly 4 zones" count, while the boolean engine's splitter emits 28 extra zero-volume slivers around the real four 6.00 mm³ zones. The measured values themselves are exactly as designed.

**Still needs physical testing** (stated plainly):
- Snap retention over time — PLA creep/fatigue under sustained 0.3 mm deflection; safe analytically, unproven.
- Real shielding-bag fit — bag bulk is modelled (1.5 mm bulge, 4.5 mm fold), not measured.
- Print-tolerance effects on the 0.3 mm capture and 0.3 mm tab-to-wall gap (assumes ±0.2 mm FDM accuracy).
- Label readability once printed (infill inside the 0.4 mm recess).
- Opening feel — dimple leverage on the tabs.
