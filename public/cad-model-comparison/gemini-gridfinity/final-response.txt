### Handover: Gridfinity Metric Socket-Head Fastener Organizer (`5 × 4` System)

#### 1. Starter Assortment & `5 × 4` Layout (`210.0 × 168.0 mm`)
The system organizes **M2, M2.5, M3, and M4** socket-head cap screws (ISO 4762), plain hex nuts (ISO 4032), and flat washers (ISO 7089) into **four 5-unit rows** (one thread family per row from front to back) built from **three reusable parametric bin types** (`1x1_dual_hw`, `2x1_triple_screw`, `2x1_dual_screw`) in a **stepped stadium height profile**:
- **Front 2 Rows (`M2` at `Y = -63.0 mm`, `M2.5` at `Y = -21.0 mm`) — `3U` Height (`25.40 mm` total)**: Shallow `13.80 mm` cavity depth below the label shelf keeps tiny `0.3–0.5 mm` washers and short `6 mm` screws within effortless fingertip reach.
- **Rear 2 Rows (`M3` at `Y = +21.0 mm`, `M4` at `Y = +63.0 mm`) — `4U` Height (`32.40 mm` total)**: Rises `7.00 mm` above the front rows so rear labels remain in direct view while providing the extra volume needed for 20 bulky `M3/M4 × 20 mm` screws and 30 `M4` hex nuts.

- **Assortment rationale**: `M3` includes all six lengths (`6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20 mm`). `M2` and `M2.5` cover `6, 8, 10, 12, 16 mm` (omitting rare `20 mm` lengths to give `12 mm` and `16 mm` wide `39.0 mm` dual compartments). `M4` covers `8, 10, 12, 16, 20 mm` (omitting `M4×6`, which has under two threads of engagement, and placing bulky `16 mm` and `20 mm` screws in `39.0 mm` dual compartments).

#### 2. Clear Spaces, Finger Access, Interfaces & Typography
- **Clear Compartment Spaces & Measured Capacities**:
  - **Front-to-back span**: `37.80 mm` total internal length with a **`22.60 mm` unobstructed vertical top opening** in front of the `15.20 mm` horizontal rear label shelf.
  - **`1×1` Dual Hardware Bin (`41.50 × 41.50 mm`)**: Two `18.10 mm` wide compartments; usable volume below shelf is `6.98 cm³` (`3U`) and `11.50 cm³` (`4U`), holding **45–300+ hex nuts** or **90–500+ flat washers** (target: 30 pcs).
  - **`2×1` Triple Screw Bin (`83.50 × 41.50 mm`)**: Three `25.60 mm` wide compartments; usable volume below shelf is `9.87 cm³` (`3U`) and `16.26 cm³` (`4U`), holding **26–120+ screws** (target: 20 pcs).
  - **`2×1` Dual Screw Bin (`83.50 × 41.50 mm`)**: Two `39.00 mm` wide compartments; usable volume below shelf is `15.03 cm³` (`3U`) and `24.77 cm³` (`4U`), holding **30–140+ long screws** (`M4×20` total length is `24.0 mm`, lying flat in both X and Y).
- **Zero-Overhang Front Scoop & Support-Free Shelf**:
  - The `1.85 mm` perimeter wall is flush with the inner vertical face of the Gridfinity stacking lip (`37.80 mm` inner span) and transitions into an `R = 11.00 mm` concave floor scoop at the front (`-Y`), so sliding a `0.3 mm` `M2` washer up the front wall with a finger meets no inward lip overhang.
  - The horizontal rear label shelf is supported underneath by a continuous `45°` ramp down to the floor/rear wall, eliminating any blind undercut where tiny washers could hide and printing without supports.
- **Permanent FillaPrint Labels & Nut/Washer Distinction**:
  - Uses `assets/fillaprint/Fillaprint-Regular.ttf` on a horizontal XY shelf (`Z = 19.60 mm` on `3U`, `Z = 26.60 mm` on `4U`, `+0.60 mm` raised relief) so FDM slicers trace each character stroke as two continuous perimeter beads (`2w`).
  - Primary thread and screw-length labels use `em = 9.00 mm` (`cap = 6.30 mm`, `stroke = 0.90 mm`, `w = 0.45 mm`); secondary `NUT` and `WASH` labels use `em = 8.60 mm` (`cap = 6.02 mm`, `stroke = 0.86 mm`, `w = 0.43 mm`), exceeding the `0.40–0.42 mm` nozzle minimums (`em = 8.00–8.40 mm`, `cap = 5.60–5.88 mm`).
  - Every `1×1` Hardware Bin pairs `NUT` (left) and `WASH` (right) with a raised **3D Hexagon Nut Icon** (`6.4 mm` across corners) on the left and a raised **3D Flat Washer Ring Icon** (`6.4 mm` OD) on the right.

#### 3. Handling, Stacking, Extension & Physical Fit Tests Needed
- **Handling & Stacking**:
  - All 12 bins sit in a single layer on `baseplate_5x4` with a `0.50 mm` gap between adjacent bins (`0.25 mm/side` foot-to-socket clearance) so any bin lifts out cleanly without disturbing neighbors.
  - Bins stack in any combination (`2×1` on `2×1`, `1×1` on `1×1`, or `1×1` on either cell of a `2×1` bin, indexed by the perimeter stacking lip and the `X = 0` front inter-foot locator rib) with `1.55 mm` vertical clearance between the raised FillaPrint text and the upper bin's foot.
- **Extending with Another Size**:
  - Add a new row entry to `STARTER_BINS` in `generate_organizer.py` using `1x1_dual_hw`, `2x1_triple_screw`, or `2x1_dual_screw` and increment `BASEPLATE_ROWS`.
- **Physical Fit Tests & Limitations**:
  1. **Baseplate & Stacking Tolerance Test**: Print one `1×1` bin (`bin_m2_nuts_washers.stl`) first to verify drop-in seating and release against your printer's first-layer elephant's foot compensation (`0.25 mm/side` nominal lateral clearance) on an existing `42 mm` Gridfinity baseplate and `baseplate_5x4.stl`.
  2. **Two-Bead Slicer Preview Check**: Slice with a `0.40 mm` nozzle at `0.20 mm` layer height and `0.40–0.44 mm` top extrusion width to confirm clean two-bead stroke fill on the top 3 label layers.
  3. **Bare-Finger vs. Gloved Access**: The `18.10 × 22.60 mm` top opening on the `1×1` Nut/Washer bin is sized for bare fingertips (`~14–16 mm` wide); gloved handling requires widening the hardware bin to `grid_x = 2`.
