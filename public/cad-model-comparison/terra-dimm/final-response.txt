The two-part mailer is complete. It has separate bays, a lowered divider for lifting out the bags, and two flexible snap tongues that release by pulling outward.

The target is a **bare DIMM up to 140 × 39 × 9 mm**, leaving room in each bay for a thin shielding bag and modest padding. The README identifies two mainstream examples, but their dimensions could not be independently verified because the specified research proxy was unreachable; measure the modules and packed bags before relying on the fit.

Both exported parts are valid single solids with watertight meshes, and the closed CAD assembly has no solid interference. The supplied FillaPrint font is used for the raised labels. **Snap retention, reopening, print tolerances, label readability, packed fit, and shipping protection still need physical testing.**
