Revision complete. The label-visibility finding is fixed by geometry and now measured on the full assembly; seating, play, stacking, neighbour clearance and fingertip access were re-run and are unchanged.

**What changed**

- Each label moved from the front edge of its compartment to the back. It is now the 35° sloped front face of a solid wedge divider, so the text stands 23 to 79 mm behind the bin's front rim instead of 1 to 7 mm.
- The 45° face became 35° and the two-line pitch dropped to the font's 18 w, lifting the lower WASHER/NUT line by 2.4 mm. The nut/washer bins give the front compartment 3 mm more length so that line clears the rim earlier.
- The wedge against the back wall stops 2 mm short of it, because the stacking lip's inward tip otherwise shadowed that label even from overhead.
- Nothing overhangs a compartment any more. The price is floor length: 18.8 mm per screw compartment, 28.5 and 22.5 mm for washers and nuts, all still above the 16 mm fingertip test.

**Remaining limitations, stated plainly**

- Below 25° elevation the front rims hide the lower text line of the nut/washer bins. From 20° to one side, the wide WASHER lines need 35° because the bin's own side wall intrudes.
- Parts pushed hard ride onto the 35° label ramp, and the text grooves (0.9 wide, 0.6 deep) are in that path. Small M2 washers lodging there is listed as a fit test.
- Wedges are solid in the CAD (46 cm³ per bin); slicer infill keeps the printed weight around 20 g. Nothing has been printed, so the fit-test list in the README still stands.
